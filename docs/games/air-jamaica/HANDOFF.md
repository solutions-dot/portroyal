## 25 November 2026, evening: flight deck, landing, cabin, last chapters
- **Flight deck rebuilt from photographs** (Wikimedia Commons: an MK Airlines DC-8, CC BY-SA; an SAS DC-8-33 of the 1960s; a DC-8 flight deck at the Firato exhibition, Dutch National Archive; the Seaboard World Airlines page's Douglas photographs of a DC-8-63's pilots' panel and flight engineer's panel). What changed: a narrow overhead panel (the middle pane's width) that drops steeply to the windscreen, with the four red fire handles at its forward end and switches standing out of its face; its labels follow Just Flight's "DC-8 Pilot's Notes" (a simulator manual, a secondary source). Mid-grey panels and a grey-blue lining, not black. A pale padded glareshield rail, the flight director's knobs, warning lights. Air outlets and dome lights in the ceiling. Engine instruments in four columns of five (EPR, N1, EGT, N2, FF) and a landing gear panel. The engineer's panel runs desk to ceiling and 1.85 m along the wall, laid out like the Douglas photograph, with four engine levers and a narrow desk with his log. Sun visors folded up against the panel above the side windows. **Still general:** the exact switches on each panel, the aft part of the engineer's panel (hydraulics, cabin air), the seats.
- **Room:** 0.9 m deeper behind the seats, so you can step back and see all three crew; the pilots' seats slid forward so their hands reach the wheels naturally.
- **Landing:** a 3 degree glide path from 2.7 km out and 113 m up over the sea; touchdown at 28.5 s on "Bump!". Both pilots hold their wheel grips through it (`reachTo`, engine/grip.js). The engineer plays `SitSwitches`.
- **Take-off (1969):** the steps roll 7 m back and stay; the door is shut (a closed-door copy of the DC-8 takes over at 5.5 s) before she moves.
- **New colours:** a 10-second camera scene first (`FILMS.reveal`, steps 38 and 39): nose, along the side, up to the blue tail.
- **Cabin (chef and last flight):** 3.9 m wide with a 1.12 m aisle, rows 1.1 m apart, 0.8 m more room at the back (a game's allowance, wider than a real A320: say so if asked). Mum sits in her seat. The windows sit beside the passengers' heads, so they show over the seat backs (the seats hid them). The cabin lining is kept off the plaster texture.
- **Box lid:** it never moved in the game because the place's merge froze it; it is now marked dynamic, and `engine/audit-reveal.js` reports any moving piece not marked `noBatch` in every game.
- **Order:** the ending music played during the last landing, before the last quiz: now only on the end card. When a chapter's quiz is done the goal says "Chapter N is finished." instead of "What did you learn?".
- **Clips:** `PullRope`, `PullStop`, `NervousLook`, `SitSwitches`, `Wave2`, `FishIdle`, `StandPoseM2` in the shared library (Mixamo, from the owner). ## Setting up the builder in a fresh container

2. MPFB: download `add-on-mpfb-v2.0.18.zip` from extensions.blender.org (index at `/api/v1/extensions/?format=json`), then in bpy: `bpy.ops.extensions.package_install_files(filepath=..., repo="user_default", enable_on_install=True)`.

## New primary source (owner, 24 November 2026): `docs/games/air-jamaica/ref-1969-ad-dc9-crew.jpg`
A 1969 Air Jamaica advertisement, "They put a little bit of Jamaica in a whole lot of plane." (source and rights unknown; reference only, never shipped). Its text says: operations started **April 1, 1969**; "the Jamaican government and Air Canada are running things"; "brand-new... sleek DC-9's"; **"Front-end crews? Air Canada is taking care of that. They're putting men in the left-hand seat"** (Air Canada captains at first); **"60 of the most beautiful girls in all of Jamaica... our Rare Tropical Birds"** (the Gleaner says 58: say "about sixty"); Jamaican music, fashion shows; seafood, beef and poultry "prepared by chefs who understand the subtle use of island spices", fine wines from Europe; First Class has "the only standup bar flying between New York and Jamaica". The picture: the long crew line stands beside a **DC-9** (not a DC-8) with boarding steps at the forward door, men in dark suits at the front, each stewardess with a **white flight bag** at her feet and a **printed scarf over the arm**, dresses in yellow, red and green with **longer sleeves and collars** than our short-sleeved minis. Done (24 November 2026, later): a white flight bag stands beside each crew member (`P.flightBag`, Aunt Marcia's too); `aj_crew2` and `aj_crew5` have longer sleeves and a turn-down collar (full sheets looked at: no cloth faults; the collar edge is a little ragged on the far copies); Captain Ross now says "Air Canada lends the new airline some captains" and "Jamaican pilots fly with us too", and the captain journal page quotes the ad. **Not changed:** the apron plane stays a DC-8, because no source says which jet flew the first flight (the ad's picture shows a DC-9, and one already stands behind).
