Everything gathered for the game, kept with the design page `docs/games/morant-bay.md` (read that first). Paths below are relative to this folder.

## Rights and how to use the images

- **Reference only, never textures** (CLAUDE.md, `docs/STANDARD.md`).
- `images/restricted/` holds copies of images from the National Library of Jamaica (permission needed to reproduce), the Bank of Jamaica ($10 note design), Yale University Library and Princeton University Library (1865 photographs; each site says use may be subject to copyright). They are here so the person who builds the faces and places has the exact pictures the owner accepted. **Do not publish them, and remove this folder before the repo is made public** unless the owner has cleared them. `docs/` is not part of the published site (`tools/dist.mjs` builds only the games).
- Other National Library of Jamaica photographs (the rebuilt courthouse, Stony Gut today) are linked from `docs/games/morant-bay.md` and not copied.
- The two `sources/` files from the Royal Commission are machine OCR of an 1866 report; the Underhill file is an 1895 book. Both are public domain. Em and en dashes in the OCR were replaced with hyphens (repo rule 1).

## Reading the sources

- Cite the report by printed page (Part I) or PDF page and question number (Part II). Check any quotation on the page image (the Google Books scan, https://books.google.com/books?id=iDUe5-p_8IAC) before it goes in the game.
- Where Underhill and the Commission differ, the Commission wins.
- A heritage leaflet at the National Library of Jamaica and the Princeton post's own narrative disagree with the Commission in places; see `notes/record-and-conflicts.md`.
