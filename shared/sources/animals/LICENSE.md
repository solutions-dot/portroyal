# Animal models

`quaternius-donkey.glb`, `quaternius-cow.glb`, `quaternius-bull.glb` and `quaternius-horse.glb` are from the
"Ultimate Animated Animal Pack" by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimateanimatedanimals.html),
released under CC0 1.0 (public domain, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No credit is required;
we credit Quaternius anyway in the games' docs.

They are the unchanged pack files (glb export), taken on 27 November 2026. `tools/animals/pack_animals.mjs` packs
them into `shared/models/`; `engine/beasts.js` and `engine/mule.js` smooth, colour, scale and animate them.
