# toigo_male_suit_3

A man's three-button suit (jacket with a short, high lapel and collar, shirt collar and front, tie, trousers, buttons) by Margaret Toigo, from the
MakeHuman community asset pack `suits01`, licence CC0 (https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/suits01.html). Only the files the
builder needs are kept: the fitting file (`.mhclo`), the mesh (`.obj`), its material and the colour texture, which is read only to tell the parts
apart (the game colours every part from the recipe). Its pocket handkerchief is left off. Fitted to a body by `tools/people/outfit.py` (see
`docs/PIPELINE.md`, "Fitted garments").

Worn since 22 November 2026 by the men of about 1908 to 1925 (both Marcus Garveys, the critic and the delegate in his game): a jacket that buttons
high with a short lapel is closer to the cut of those years than the two-button suit (`toigo_male_suit_tie_and_jacket`), which the men of 1948
and later keep. It is still a modern pattern, not a copy of a 1914 jacket, and its shirt has a turn-down collar, not a stiff stand collar.
