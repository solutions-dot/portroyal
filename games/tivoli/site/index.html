What happened

In May 2010, soldiers and police went into Tivoli Gardens in Kingston.

They wanted to arrest a man the United States wanted.

Gunfire went on for hours.

About 70 civilians died. Civilians are people who are not soldiers or police.

One soldier died too.

People still disagree about what happened.

Soldiers and police said armed men fought them.

Many neighbours said they never saw armed men inside.
