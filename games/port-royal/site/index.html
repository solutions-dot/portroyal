Port Royal, 1690 The story of Lewis Galdy

It is 1690. You are Lewis Galdy, a young man from France. Your ship has just landed at Port Royal, the busiest, wildest town in the Caribbean.

Nobody knows it yet, but in two years an earthquake will swallow this town. And you will live to tell the tale.

Where the history comes from

  • The tombstone of Lewis Galdy, St Peter’s Church, Port Royal (1739)
  • Letters of the Rev. Emmanuel Heath, and a London news sheet, both from 1692
  • The Port Royal Project of the Institute of Nautical Archaeology and Texas A&M University, led by Donny L. Hamilton
  • Shirley Gotelipe-Miller, Pewter and Pewterers from Port Royal, Jamaica (1990)
  • Michael Pawson and David Buisseret, Port Royal, Jamaica
  • Nuala Zahedieh, on the merchants of Port Royal
  • Reconstruction paintings of the town, used as a guide for the houses
  • The National Museum of Jamaica and the Jamaica National Heritage Trust

Abba, Kofi, Sam and the camp survivors are fictional. Lewis Galdy, Symon Benning and Emmanuel Heath were real people, but their words in this game are fictional.
